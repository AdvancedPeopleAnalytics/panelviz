# panelviz: Import, Aggregate and Visualise Any Panel Data

Visualise any panel data (also called longitudinal data, repeated
measures or time-series cross-section: the same individuals observed
over time). Declare which column is the individual and which is time;
the id and time formats are recognised (dates, years, quarters, months,
ISO weeks, Stata date formats, plain indices), the data is aggregated in
two stages (within individual over time, then across individuals by
group), and publication-quality charts are drawn. Generalises the
plotting approach of 'wpa' and 'vivainsights'.
